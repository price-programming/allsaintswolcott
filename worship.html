Sundays at 9:30 AM

Worship at All Saints'

We gather each week around scripture, prayer, music, and Holy Communion—and there is always room for you.

Holy Eucharist

Our Sunday service follows the Episcopal liturgy and is led by Deacon CJ and the Rev. Andy Zeiman, our supply priest for 2026. Printed materials help everyone follow along.

Come as you are

You do not need to know the words, belong to the parish, or have a particular background. You may join in as you feel comfortable. Families and children are always welcome.

Holy Communion

All baptized Christians are welcome to receive Communion. If you would rather receive a blessing or remain in your seat, either choice is completely welcome.

After the service

Coffee fellowship follows in the parish hall. Stay for a cup and conversation—we would be glad to meet you.
