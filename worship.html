Our Sunday Service

Holy Eucharist is celebrated every Sunday at 10:00 AM in our sanctuary — the small red chapel on Bound Line Road that's welcomed this parish for generations. The service is led by our clergy, currently Deacon CJ and Father Andy Zeiman, our supply priest for 2026.

What to Expect

Come as you are. The service includes hymns, scripture, a sermon, and Holy Communion, in the traditional Episcopal liturgy. Families with children are always welcome, and there's no need to worry about a little noise — this is a family-friendly congregation from infants to ninety-two.

After the Service

Stay for coffee fellowship in the parish hall, where the conversation is as much a part of Sunday morning as the service itself.
