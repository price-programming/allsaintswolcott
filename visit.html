Plan your visit

Your first Sunday can be simple.

Worship begins at 9:30 AM. Come as you are, take any open seat, and participate as much or as little as you like.

What to expect

Our service includes hymns, scripture, a sermon, prayer, and Holy Communion in the Episcopal tradition. The service is warm and welcoming, whether you have worshiped in an Episcopal church for years or have never attended one before.

Families are welcome

Children are part of our church family, and a little noise is never a problem. There is no dress code; wear what makes you comfortable.

After worship

Stay for coffee and light refreshments in the parish hall. It is an easy way to meet people and ask any questions you may have.

Need help before you arrive?

Email allsaintswolcott@gmail.com or call (203) 879-2800. We will be glad to help.
