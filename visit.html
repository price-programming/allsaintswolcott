Plan Your Visit

Join us on a Sunday morning

Everything you need to know before your first visit — service times, what to expect, and how to find us.

Service Times

Sunday worship begins at 10:00 AM and is followed by coffee fellowship in the parish hall. Our worship includes Holy Eucharist, and services are warm, welcoming, and open to all — whether you've worshipped with us for decades or you're walking in for the first time.

What to Expect

Come as you are — there's no dress code here. Families with young children are always welcome, and there's no need to worry about a little noise during the service. If you're new, feel free to introduce yourself to a greeter at the door, or simply find a seat and settle in.

The Prayer Garden

Our prayer garden is open to the public as a quiet space for reflection, any day of the week — you don't need to wait for a service to visit.
