Sundays after worship

Coffee fellowship

The conversation after church is as much a part of Sunday morning as the service itself.

Gather in the parish hall

After worship, the congregation moves into the parish hall for coffee, light refreshments, and conversation. It is an informal way to catch up with friends and welcome new faces.

New here?

Coffee hour is a comfortable place to introduce yourself and ask questions about the parish. There is no formality and no cost—simply come in and join us.

Help host

If you would like to help with a future coffee hour, email allsaintswolcott@gmail.com.
