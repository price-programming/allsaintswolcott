Sundays, After the 10 AM Service

Coffee Fellowship

The conversation after church is as much a part of Sunday morning as the service itself.

Gather in the Parish Hall

Once the service ends, the congregation moves into the parish hall for coffee, light refreshments, and conversation. It's an easy, informal way to meet people, catch up with old friends, and get to know newcomers.

New Here?

Coffee hour is the best place to introduce yourself — there's no formality to it, and members are always glad to welcome a new face and answer questions about the parish.
