Music and service

Choir and outreach

We share our gifts in worship and put faith into action through care for our neighbors.

Join the choir

Our choir helps lead music during Sunday worship. New voices are welcome, regardless of experience. If you enjoy singing, we would be glad to help you learn more.

Serve the community

We collect non-perishable food items for the Wolcott Food Pantry every month, as well as recyclable cans and bottles that fund our Altmeyer Scholarship for the students of Wolcott Public Schools.

Take a first step

Email allsaintswolcott@gmail.com and tell us whether you are interested in music, outreach, or both.
