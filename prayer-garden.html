The prayer garden

A quiet place on the church grounds for stillness, reflection, and prayer.

A space set apart

Tucked among the shade trees on our grounds, the prayer garden offers benches, a central path, and a peaceful place to pause.

Open to everyone

You do not need to be a parish member or wait for a Sunday service to visit. Come whenever you need a quiet place to sit, pray, or simply be still.

Finding the garden

The garden is located on the church grounds at 282 Bound Line Road. If you need help before visiting, call (203) 879-2800.
