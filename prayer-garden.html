The Prayer Garden

← Back to Home Plan a visit →

A Space for Quiet and Reflection

Tucked among the shade trees on our grounds, the prayer garden is a place set apart for stillness. Stone benches are arranged around a central path, with an angel statue at its heart and flowering beds along the way.

Open to Everyone

You don't need to be a member of the parish, or wait for a service, to visit. The garden is open to the public any day of the week, whenever you need a quiet place to sit, pray, or simply be still.
