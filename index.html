Welcome

We are children of God who range in age from infants to ninety-two. We are people who seek to follow Jesus as best we can through prayer and service to others.

We hope you will find a warm home in Christ here. Come to a service, join the choir, help with an outreach activity, or simply spend time in our prayer garden whenever you'd like — it's open to the public as a space for quiet and reflection.

We invite you to join us on Sundays, and we hope to see you soon.
