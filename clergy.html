Clergy and parish leadership

Meet the people entrusted with guiding worship, ministry, and the shared life of All Saints'.

Our clergy

Deacon CJ serves as our permanent deacon, and the Rev. Andy Zeiman serves as our supply priest for 2026. Together they lead worship and support the pastoral life of the parish.

Wardens and officers

Mary Pepides serves as Senior Warden, Donna Lydem as Clerk, and Kathy Sullivan as Treasurer.

Vestry

Nancy Renner, Carol Reeves, Nancy Bounocore, and Debbie Price serve on the Vestry, helping steward the mission and practical life of All Saints'.

To contact a member of the leadership team, email allsaintswolcott@gmail.com.
